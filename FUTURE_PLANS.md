# Future plans for Chloris

Chloris currently maps medicinal-use plants native to **Greece**. The current catalogue, observations and risk signals are specific to that scope. The plans below describe future work, not features or coverage available today.

## Expand beyond Greece

We plan to extend the map to other countries and, eventually, make Chloris a global plant atlas. Each new region would need its own verified species list, observation data, land boundaries and conservation context. We would show where data is missing instead of treating a lack of records as proof that a plant is absent or safe.

## Grow the plant catalogue

We plan to add more species and observations as suitable datasets become available. Names and ranges would be checked against botanical sources, and photos would be added only when their identification, licence and attribution can be verified. The database should keep source and date information so users can see where each claim came from.

## Review conservation guidance

We want botanists and conservation specialists to review the draft risk method and plant guidance before broader use. Any global expansion must continue to protect precise locations of threatened plants. We also plan to improve sightings through expert verification and clearer data-quality labels.

The [README](README.md) explains what the current Greece-focused prototype actually does and where its data comes from.
